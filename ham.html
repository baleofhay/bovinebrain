HF Band Plan Quick Reference

Amateur Radio HF bands for long-distance communication (US band allocations)

160m

1.8 - 2.0 MHz

Night-time DX, local ragchewing

80m

3.5 - 4.0 MHz

Regional and DX, popular for nets

40m

7.0 - 7.3 MHz

Day & night DX, most popular band

30m

10.1 - 10.15 MHz

CW & digital only, no voice

20m

14.0 - 14.35 MHz

Premier DX band, worldwide contacts

17m

18.068 - 18.168 MHz

Less crowded, good DX

15m

21.0 - 21.45 MHz

Excellent DX during solar peaks

12m

24.89 - 24.99 MHz

Hidden gem for DX

10m

28.0 - 29.7 MHz

Long-range when open, FM simplex

VHF/UHF Band Plan Quick Reference

Amateur Radio VHF and UHF bands for local and regional communication (US band allocations)

6m

50.0 - 54.0 MHz

VHF DX, skip propagation, seasonal band

2m

144.0 - 148.0 MHz

Most popular VHF band, FM simplex, repeaters

1.25m

222.0 - 225.0 MHz

Digital modes, weak signal, limited repeaters

70cm

420.0 - 450.0 MHz

Most popular UHF band, repeaters, digital modes

33cm

902.0 - 928.0 MHz

Digital modes, weak signal, limited repeaters

23cm

1240.0 - 1300.0 MHz

Digital modes, weak signal, limited use
